Dial 211 — the master key

Free, confidential, 24 hours a day, in your own language. Local specialists who know the food, shelter, housing, reentry, and health help in your town. Text your ZIP code to 898211. If 211 won't dial through: 1-844-872-4681.

🍞 Food

National Hunger Hotline

Run for the USDA by Hunger Free America. Meal sites, food banks, and services near your address. Mon–Fri, 8 AM–8 PM Eastern. Spanish: 1-877-8-HAMBRE (1-877-842-6273). After hours, text food + your ZIP to 914-342-7744.

📞 1-866-3-HUNGRY

🕊️ Recovery — addiction & mental health

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free, confidential, 24/7, every day of the year — treatment referrals and information for substance use and mental health, English and Spanish. TTY: 1-800-487-4889. Or text your ZIP code to 435748 (HELP4U).

💡 Bills, lights & basics

Keep the lights and heat on

Utility help exists in nearly every county — energy assistance (LIHEAP), Salvation Army and church benevolence funds, and payment plans you have to ask for before shutoff, not after. Dial 211 and say "utility assistance" — they know who still has funds this month, because these pots open and close fast.

📞 Call 211

👶 Families & children

Moms, babies & little ones

WIC feeds pregnant moms, new mothers, and children under 5 — dial 211 and ask for WIC in your county. Diaper banks give free diapers through the National Diaper Bank Network.

Find diapers →

No ride?

Say so when you call. Many pantries run mobile stops or can arrange delivery for shut-ins, and 211 knows which rides and bus programs run in your town. Don't let a ride be the reason you go without — ask.

📞 Call 211

🔓 Coming home — prison & reentry

Coming home is hard enough without a map. Start with 211 and findhelp.org and ask for reentry services in your county — IDs, housing, jobs, and programs that work with people coming home — and ask about ID help: replacing a birth certificate, Social Security card, or state ID is the first lock reentry programs can open, because nothing else moves without one. Still Standing knows this road first-hand: our founder came home and stood back up, and our survivor and reentry work exists so the next man or woman doesn't have to figure it out alone. Central voice: (251) 348-3677.
